    Roof Types

    Every system, one crew.

    Explore the roofing systems we install, repair, and restore across the Gulf Coast.

    Asphalt Shingle Roofing

    Asphalt roofing shingles are the most popular and cost-effective roofing material designed to protect a home from the elements.

    Architectural Shingles

    These dimensional shingles offer enhanced durability and aesthetic depth. A top choice for homeowners looking for curb appeal with longevity.

    Metal Roofing

    Corrugated and standing seam metal roofs offer long-lasting performance and structural strength for warehouses and retail spaces.

    Metal Roofing

    Metal roofing is a broad category including Aluminum, Copper, Standing Seam, and Stone-coated Steel. These materials can last two to three times longer than others.

    TPO Roofing

    TPO roofs are top-rated for energy efficiency and UV resistance. Durable and flexible, they're ideal for flat commercial roofs.

    PVC Roofing

    PVC membranes offer high chemical resistance, fire performance, and waterproofing for demanding commercial environments.

    EPDM Roofing

    Thermoset EPDM membranes are synthetic rubber systems known for their resistance to UV rays, weathering, and temperature changes.

    Silicone Coating

    Silicone restoration membranes extend the life of commercial roofs while offering excellent weatherproofing and UV protection.

    Modified Bitumen

    Reinforced asphalt membranes designed for flexibility and durability on low-slope commercial roofs in all weather conditions.

    Torch Down Roofing

    Ideal for flat roofs, torch down roofing melts modified bitumen onto the roof deck for a watertight, durable membrane.

    Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

    Multiple layers of bitumen and reinforcing felt create a time-tested waterproofing system with strong resistance to damage.
